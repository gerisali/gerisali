# XR for neurorehabilitation

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

**Collaborators:** Daniele Romano and Giorgia Tosi, University of Milano-Bicocca, within COST Action CA24123<br>
**Status:** Prototype development

Two mixed-reality prototypes for Meta Quest 3, developed with clinical neuropsychology
partners. Both are research prototypes: they are not diagnostic or medical devices, and their
parameters are still open to clinical review.

## 360° visual assessment for spatial neglect
A passthrough mixed-reality task that measures whether a person notices static visual targets
placed all around them (front, back, left, right), not only in front. The output is a
detection map by region, intended to support the assessment of spatial neglect.

## Guided hand training
The user sees their real hand through passthrough while a semi-transparent reference hand
demonstrates the movement. The session moves from preview to guided execution, detects
mismatches, gives rule-based feedback and ends with a summary. The first version covers
hand open–close, wrist flexion–extension and finger-to-thumb sequences. It builds on
Romano et al. (2021), *Neuropsychologia*, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2021.107964.
