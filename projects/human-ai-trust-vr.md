# Human–AI trust in VR

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

**Collaborators:** Günter Alce, Lund University<br>
**Status:** Paper under review

## What it is
When an AI agent in VR speaks fluently and quickly, people tend to rely on it. This project
studies how users' performance with a VR AI agent relates to how much they trust it, and
whether a smooth, confident agent can lead people to trust it more than its actual help
warrants.

## Questions we look at
- Does better task performance with an agent go together with higher trust, or can the two
  drift apart?
- Which qualities of the agent (fluency, speed, voice) shape trust in immersive settings?
- What does this mean for designing AI agents for training and work in VR?
