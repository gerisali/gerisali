## What it is
EVRECA is an educational virtual reality campus for skill-based learning. It was designed,
built and evaluated over two cycles of educational design research.

## Main findings
- Training in EVRECA supported learning at both the knowledge and the skill level, and what
  was learned transferred to real-life tasks.
- A stronger sense of presence went together with lower cognitive load and with higher
  pleasure and arousal.
- Technical factors such as frame rate, latency, draw calls and polygon count noticeably
  affected the experience.
