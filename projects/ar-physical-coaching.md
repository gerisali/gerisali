# AR physical coaching

[← Back to profile](../README.md)

**Collaborators:** Johannes Nivala, Viktor Månsson and Günter Alce, Lund University<br>
**Status:** Published (ICDVRAT 2026)

## What it is
An augmented-reality coach that watches a user's posture during exercise and gives feedback.
Body tracking comes from a Meta Quest 3 and an iPhone. A local machine-learning model
classifies posture, and an LLM turns the result into short spoken feedback. The study compares
what the local model and the LLM each contribute and looks at users' workload.
