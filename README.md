# Ali Geriş

Associate Professor · Computer and Instructional Technologies Education, Faculty of Education, Manisa Celal Bayar University, Türkiye<br>
Affiliated Researcher · Lund University, Sweden

I work at the intersection of extended reality (XR) and AI. My recent research looks at
voice- and vision-capable LLM agents inside XR: how fast they need to be, how they see what
the user sees, and how much people trust them. Alongside this, I build XR prototypes for
rehabilitation with clinical partners in Europe, and I have a long-standing interest in
immersive learning.
